Architecture

Between DONE and master, a recompute

The mission orchestrator keeps a mission moving until a run reports DONE. The merge gate is the landing half: it sits between that report and master and re-derives the verdict from three stores — the mission ledger, the run-state on disk, and the diff being landed. Nothing is taken from the run's own summary.

Sorry-delta

Apologies are admissions, not summaries

The sorry-delta scans two surfaces: the added lines of the diff (removed lines never count — deleting a TODO is progress) and the run's declared phase summaries. Two marker families:

FamilyMarkersWhereExempt
stubtodo!(), NotImplementedError, TODO/FIXME, placeholder implementationsadded lines, non-test filestest/ and *.test.ts — seams are stubbed by design
apologysorry, I couldn't, unable to complete, cannot fulfilladded lines + run summariesnowhere — an apology in a summary is the run saying it did not finish

The summaries are scanned in the opposite direction of trust: they are never evidence of completion, only evidence against it.

Axiom-honesty

A PR may not weaken the gates that judge it

The diff is matched against the gate system's own paths: enforcer hooks, CI workflows, policy enforcers, the quality baseline, the version-bump script, capacity policy — and the merge gate and queue themselves. Touching any of them fails the check unless the change is acknowledged in plain sight: a gate-infra PR label, or --allow-gate-infra on the CLI. A reviewer sees the label; the gate sees the diff.

◆ no escape

There is no environment switch. UAP_*_OFF is deliberately not consulted, and the queue's --force skips stale CI checks but never this gate — a force flag is exactly the gate-off escape axiom-honesty exists to refuse. This page's own PR was itself gated, under its own gate-infra label.
